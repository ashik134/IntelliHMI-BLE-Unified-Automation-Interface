Model architecture: MobileFaceNet (Chen et al., 2018), 112x112x3 RGB
input, 192-dimensional output embedding, pixel normalization
(pixel - 128) / 128.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRE-PRODUCTION GATE — READ BEFORE ENABLING REAL OPERATOR ENROLLMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This model's training-data provenance is not documented by its source
repository. Models in this MobileFaceNet/InsightFace lineage are
commonly trained on MS-Celeb-1M and/or CASIA-WebFace — large, web-scraped
face datasets assembled without the depicted individuals' consent for
this purpose. Microsoft withdrew MS-Celeb-1M in 2019 specifically over
this concern. This is standard practice across the mobile face-
recognition ecosystem (no well-documented, mobile-ready alternative with
cleaner provenance exists as of the plan that added this file), but it
is a real compliance/legal consideration, not a formality.

Do not enable real operator face enrollment or matching against real
users in a production deployment until this has been through your own
legal/compliance review (data-protection regulation applicable to your
deployment, biometric-data-specific statutes where relevant, and your
organization's own AI/ML sourcing policy). This model is bundled so the
detection/liveness/matching pipeline is genuinely testable during
development — that is not the same as being cleared for production use
on real people.
